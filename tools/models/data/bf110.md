# Bf 110C — researched dimensions, sources and station tables

Built by `tools/models/data/bf110.py` (`build()`, no args). Units: metres; nose **+Y**, up **+Z**,
right **+X**. Origin at mid-length, level attitude, gear down. Deterministic — no RNG.
Twin DB 601 engines in wing-root nacelles, **twin fins** on the tailplane tips, Zerstörer nose.

Height = fin top above the main-wheel contact plane, level attitude (propellers excluded from the
bounding box). Wikipedia's C-1 spec sheet lists 4.128 m (13 ft 6½ in, from Green's *Warplanes of the
Third Reich*); airpages echoes 4.13 m, while warbirdsresourcegroup, the German-language tables and
most scale drawings give 3.50/3.51 m. The disagreement is the tail-down parked attitude plus
prop clearance in the taller reading; the 3.50 m airframe figure is used and flagged here.

## Nodes emitted

`Body` > `Fuselage`, `Wing`, `Tailplane`, `Fin` (both fins in one mesh), `Canopy`,
`NacelleL`, `NacelleR`; top level: **`Propeller`** > `Blades` + `Spinner` + `PropDisc` (port),
`PropellerR` (starboard), `GearL`, `GearR`, `Pilot`, `CockpitEye`, `MuzzleL`, `MuzzleR`,
`CockpitMount`, `Decals`. Extras: `GunTop0/1` + `GunTopB0/1` (4 × MG 17), `GunFF0/1` (2 × MG FF),
`Radiator0/1`, `Exhaust0/1`, `TailWheel`, `TailLeg`, and a second fin band `DecalFinBandR`
(`kit.markings` only paints one fin).

## Known compromises

* **Only the port propeller spins in game.** `scripts/aircraft.gd` does
  `_model.find_child("Propeller", true, false)` and rotates that single node, so a twin needs two
  nodes; the starboard set is the separate `PropellerR` node. `kit.propeller()` has no nacelle
  offset parameter, so its local origin is re-placed to x = ±2.20 after the call.
* Wheel track / engine centres / tailplane span / fin and tail chords are all scaled from a
  three-view — no dimensional table for the Bf 110 exists in the sources found.
* The 4.128 m height reading is documented above but not built to.
* No swastika anywhere.
