# Junkers Ju 87 B Stuka — dimensions & authoring stations

Type: single-engine dive bomber (Ju 87 B, Jumo 211D, inverted-gull cranked wing). Nation: Luftwaffe.
Authored in metres, nose toward +Y, up +Z, right +X (glTF export maps +Y onto Godot's -Z).

Gear track, wheel diameter, root/tip chords and the exact crank station are scaled off published 3-views
(the cited sources give span/length/height/area).
