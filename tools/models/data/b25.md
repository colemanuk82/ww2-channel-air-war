# North American B-25J Mitchell — 1:1 model dimensions

Geometry: `tools/models/data/b25.py` (build() — kit.py primitives only). Metres, nose toward +Y,
origin at the CG station; the glTF export maps Blender +Y onto Godot −Z.

Type modelled: **B-25J**, glazed bombardier nose, **twin fin-and-rudder (H-tail)**, Bendix dorsal
turret, twin-gun tail turret, two waist guns, two fixed nose guns, tricycle gear with the main
wheels under the nacelles.

> Note: the brief grouped the twins as "twin (A-20) and single (B-25/B-26) fins". That is backwards —
> the B-25 is the twin-finned type; the A-20 and B-26 both have a single fin. Built historically.
