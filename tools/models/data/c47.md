# Douglas C-47 Skytrain / Dakota — researched dimensions and station tables

Model id `c47` · source module `tools/models/data/c47.py` · built by
`blender --background --python tools/models/build_all.py -- c47`

All geometry is authored from the published figures below; nothing is converted from a
third-party mesh. Frame: metres, origin at the CG station (25 % MAC), **nose toward +Y**,
up +Z, right +X (the glTF export maps that onto Godot's −Z forward). Everything is
deterministic — no RNG — so a rebuild is byte-identical.

## Headline dimensions (gated at 2 %)

| Quantity | Published | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Length (overall, nose to rudder trailing edge) | 19.66 m (64 ft 5 in) | https://aeropedia.com.au/content/douglas-dc-3-c-47 · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_DC-3 |
| Wingspan | 28.96 m (95 ft 0 in) | https://aeropedia.com.au/content/douglas-dc-3-c-47 · http://flugzeuginfo.net/acdata_php/acdata_dc3_en.php |
| Height (level attitude, ground to fin top) | 5.16 m (16 ft 9 in) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_DC-3 · https://aeropedia.com.au/content/douglas-dc-3-c-47 |
| Wing area | 91.7 m² (987 sq ft; NACA TN 3088 gives 988.9 sq ft) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_DC-3 · http://flugzeuginfo.net/acdata_php/acdata_dc3_en.php |
| Aspect ratio | 9.13 / 9.17 | NACA TN 3088 Table I · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_DC-3 |

The three headline figures are set by construction, so the numeric gate is structural rather
than cosmetic: the rounded nose tip sits at `+6.60 m` and the rudder trailing edge at
`−13.06 m` (**length**), the outer wing stations reach exactly `±14.48 m` (**span**), and the
main-wheel treads are placed 0.06 m below the propeller-tip arc — the lowest point in the level
attitude — with the fin top then solved so `z_max − z_min = 5.16 m` (**height**). See
`_accuracy()` in the module.

Notes on the published figures (sources disagree, as they do for every DC-3/C-47 family): aeropedia
and the Douglas DC-3 site give length 19.66 m (64 ft 5½ in); Wikipedia and flugzeuginfo give
19.65–19.7 m (64 ft 5 in) for the same airframe. Height is quoted either as 5.16 m (16 ft 9 in
"level attitude") or 5.16–5.17 m (16 ft 11½ in); Wikipedia's separate 23 ft 6 in figure is the
**tail-down parked** height, which is not the figure a level-built model can be gated against.

Cross-checks: the crew-chief notes for a flying DC-3 quote "props are 11½ feet across" (agrees
with the 3.505 m propeller) and "main gear is about 20 feet across the wheels"
(https://www.enginehistory.org/Operations/DC-3CrewChiefNotes.pdf) — an 18 ft 6 in centre-to-centre
track plus a ~0.5 m tyre width gives ~20 ft outer-to-outer, so the 5.64 m track figure is the
consistent reading. A general Q&A page quotes a ~14 ft "track"; the Douglas/C-47 service manual
figure is used instead. Wing area 91.7 m² and span 28.96 m give a mean chord of 3.17 m, matching
the independent 3-view measurement at https://aircraftinvestigation.info/airplanes/Douglas_DC-3.html.

### Two quantities that are derived, not directly published

* **Nacelle / engine centreline (`x = ±3.20 m`).** Not tabulated in any source I could find. It is
  fixed by two hard constraints: (a) the 3.505 m propeller disc must clear the fuselage, whose
  half-width at the propeller plane is ~1.31 m, so the nacelle centre must sit outboard of
  ≈3.06 m — this is why the real DC-3 prop tips pass so close to the fuselage; and (b) the main
  wheels retract into the nacelles at the 5.64 m track (x = ±2.82 m). 3.20 m satisfies both and
  places the nacelle on the constant-chord centre section, whose computed half-width is 3.81 m.
  Some 3-view readings suggest the nacelles look a little further outboard; I could not confirm
  that against a digitised drawing, so the physically constrained value is used.
* **tip chord 47.6 in.** The NACA table lists 170 in root, 138.1 in MAC and a 56 in chord 30 in
  inboard of the tip. Solving the two-segment (constant centre section + linear outer taper)
  planform for the published area (988.9 sq ft) and MAC yields a centre section 150.1 in wide
  tapering to 47.6 in at the construction tip; back-substituting gives 56.4 in at the 30-in
  station, matching the table. The model's tip is then rounded off over its outer 0.6 m, so the
  physical tip chord is smaller than 1.209 m.

Plus a `DorsalFin` fillet lofted on the tail-cone spine ahead of the fin, and a small `Astrodome`
blister on the cabin roof 0.17 m behind the cockpit.

**Gear** — tailwheel (not tricycle): main legs `GearL/GearR` at `x = ±2.82 m` (18 ft 6 in track),
pivot `y = 1.53`, `z = −0.35`, 0.635 m tyres; the leg length is solved at build time so the wheel
tread is the lowest point. Fixed `TailWheel` at `y = −10.9`.

**Contract nodes emitted:** `Body`, `Fuselage`, `Wing`, `Tailplane`, `Fin`, `Canopy` (+
`CanopyFrame*`, `Windscreen*`), `Pilot`, `Propeller` > `Blades` + `Spinner` + `PropDisc` and
`PropellerR` > `BladesR` + `SpinnerR` + `PropDiscR` (two 3-blade props), `GearL`, `GearR`,
`TailWheel`, `CockpitEye`, `CockpitMount`, `MuzzleL`, `MuzzleR`, `Decals`
(`DecalFusBlue/White_*`, `DecalWingBlue/WhiteU_*`). Extras: `Nacelle`/`NacelleR`,
`Cowling`/`CowlingR`, `Exhaust*`, `Intake*`, `DorsalFin`, `Astrodome`, `CargoDoor`, `CrewDoor`,
`Hatch`, `GearHub`, `Leg`, `Wheel`.

**Armament:** none — the C-47 is an unarmed transport. The `MuzzleL`/`MuzzleR` nodes exist only
because Godot's model contract looks for them (`Aircraft.get_muzzles()`); they are placed at the
nose as inert markers, not guns.
