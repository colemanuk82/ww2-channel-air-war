## Gear, prop, cooling, markers

* Main gear pivots `GearL/GearR` at x = ±1.975 (tread 3.95), y = 0.47, z = −0.52; leg solved so the
  0.432 m tyres land on the height-gate ground line.
* Tail wheel `TailWheel` at y = TAIL+1.30, 0.36 m tyre, on a telescoping-style strut.
* Propeller: 4 blades, radius 1.98, spinner r 0.42.
* Cooling: chin `OilCooler` scoop under the cowl + shallow `TurboDuct` belly fairing;
  six `ExhaustL/ExhaustR` stacks per side.
* `MuzzleL/MuzzleR` at (±2.80, 0.10, −0.05) (four .50s per wing); `CockpitEye`/`CockpitMount` at
  (0, −0.15, 0.90). Decals via `kit.markings("USAAF", ...)`.

## Notes / caveats

* `kit.radiator(...)` uses `mouth=False` (the kit's intake plate places the mouth at z = x — an
  argument-order slip in the shared kit); the housings read correctly on their own.
* Height is the standing (gear-down) figure; the model is posed level so the length/span bbox gate stays
  clean.
* Main gear tread is the one derived value (marked above); everything else is a published figure.
