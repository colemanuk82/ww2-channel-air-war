## Modelled configuration (type specifics)

* **THREE engines**: one in the nose (`Propeller`) plus one per wing (`Propeller2`, `Propeller3`).
* **Corrugated skin**: the fuselage section is rippled around the circumference (16 waves on a 34-gon).
* **Fixed, spatted main gear** (gear legs + wheel spats), single fin, low cantilever wing.
