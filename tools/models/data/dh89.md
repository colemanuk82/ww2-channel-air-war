# de Havilland DH.89A Dragon Rapide (civil) — 1:1 model dimensions

Geometry: `tools/models/data/dh89.py` (build() — kit.py primitives only). Metres, nose toward +Y.
In game the aircraft is the procedural `ModelSpecs.dh89()` (the glb is the authored reference).

Type modelled: **DH.89A**, civil silver finish with blue cheat line, registration G-AFRW,
twin Gipsy Queen engines in tapered nacelles, fixed spatted main wheels, tailwheel, twin fins and rudders on the
tailplane, cabin windows along both sides, two-bay biplane wings with the upper wing the longer.

Sources: Wikipedia, *De Havilland Dragon Rapide*; The de Havilland Aircraft Museum, DH.89A page; Shuttleworth
Collection, DH.89 Dragon Rapide; FlightGear wiki, DH.89 data.

Note: the Rapide is flown in the POW escape missions as a what-if. Real clandestine pick-ups from occupied Europe
used Westland Lysanders and Lockheed Hudsons of 161 Squadron.
