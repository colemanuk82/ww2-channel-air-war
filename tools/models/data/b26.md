# Martin B-26B/C Marauder (long-wing "B-10" airframe) — 1:1 model dimensions

Geometry: `tools/models/data/b26.py` (build() — kit.py primitives only). Metres, nose toward +Y,
origin at the CG station; the glTF export maps Blender +Y onto Godot −Z.

Type modelled: **B-26B-10 / B-26C** (the long-wing, taller-fin combat Marauder), plexiglas bombardier
nose, **single tall fin-and-rudder**, Martin 250CE dorsal turret, twin-gun tail turret, two waist guns,
two fixed nose guns and two "package" guns, tricycle gear with the main wheels under the under-slung
nacelles, **4-blade** props.

| Tip chord | 1.55 m (modelled, rounded) | as above; the B-26B-10 tip chord grew with the +6 ft span extension (https://ww2aircraft.net/forum/threads/b-26-engineering-data-technical-information-from-martins-own-blueprints.59449/) |
