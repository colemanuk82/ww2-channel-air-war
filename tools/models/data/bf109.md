# Bf 109E-4 — researched dimensions, sources and station tables

Built by `tools/models/data/bf109.py` (`build()`, no args). Units: metres; nose **+Y**, up **+Z**,
right **+X** (the glTF export maps this onto Godot's −Z forward). Origin at mid-length, level
flight attitude, gear down. Deterministic — no RNG.

**Caveat on height.** Published Bf 109E heights disagree: 2.50 m (wardrawings, and the figure the
task text used) vs 3.40 m (11 ft 2 in, e.g. <https://bob.plord.net/Ships/Aircraft/Germany/Bf109E.html>
and <https://wehrmacht-history.com/luftwaffe/fighters/messerschmitt-bf-109-e-fighter.html>). The
larger figure only appears when the *propeller* is included or the tail-down parked attitude is
measured. This model uses the 2.50 m airframe figure and defines height as **fin top minus main-wheel
contact plane in level attitude**; the propeller is excluded from the bounding box (its diameter is a
separate published dimension — the blur disc alone is 3.10 m across and would otherwise dominate).

## Nodes emitted

`Body` > `Fuselage`, `Wing` (single mirrored object), `Tailplane`, `Fin`, `Canopy`;
top level: `Propeller` > `Blades` + `Spinner` + `PropDisc`, `GearL`, `GearR`, `Pilot`,
`CockpitEye`, `MuzzleL`, `MuzzleR`, `CockpitMount`, `Decals`. Extras: `GunCowlL/R`,
`GunWingL/R` (two MG 17 over the engine, two MG FF in the wings), `Exhaust0/1`, `Radiator0/1`,
`OilCooler`, `Intake`, `TailWheel`, `TailLeg`.

## Known compromises

* Length is pinned to the **spinner tip**, span to the wing tips, height to the fin top (see caveat).
* `kit.markings("Luftwaffe", …)` names the fuselage crosses `DecalFus1.0_*` / `DecalFus0.72_*`
  (it interpolates the numeric scale, not the colour word), so `gltf_models._decal_material` falls
  through to its default grey for those. That is a shared-kit quirk (kit.py is not ours to edit);
  the wing crosses and the fin band do pick up black. No swastika is modelled anywhere.
* Tailplane span, fin chords and dihedral are scaled from three-views — no dimensional table found.
