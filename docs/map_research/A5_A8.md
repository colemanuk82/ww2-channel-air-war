# A5-A8 Walcheren, Market Garden, Westwall and the Rhine, 1940 anti-invasion

## A5 Walcheren
Wikipedia - Battle of the Scheldt; Operation Infatuate (sea walls at Westkapelle, Flushing and Veere breached by the RAF
3-17 Oct 1944). Flood zone (kind 5) over the island below +2.5 m from 1944-10-03. Also the Betuwe (Rhine dyke breached
near Elst, 2 Dec 1944) and the Wieringermeer (17 Apr 1945).

## A6 Arnhem, Nijmegen
Wikipedia - Battle of Arnhem; Operation Market Garden. Landing zones S, Z, X, L and dropping zones X, Y (Renkum and
Ginkel heaths, Wolfheze, Johannahoeve); Groesbeek and Son for the American divisions. The Arnhem road bridge's northern
ramp (Graebner's column, 18 Sep) as a wreck site; the bridge itself drops its centre span from 7 Oct 1944 (bombed by
the USAAF). Oosterbeek perimeter crater field.

## A7 Westwall and the Rhine
Wikipedia - Siegfried Line; Dragon's teeth (fortification); Battle of Aachen; Battle of Huertgen Forest; Operation
Veritable; Operation Varsity; Ludendorff Bridge (captured 7 Mar 1945, collapsed 17 Mar). The Scharnhorst-line teeth from
Kalterherberg / Monschau north by Roetgen, Schmidthof and Brand towards Verlautenheide (WestwallKit), streamed in ~5 km
sections: 20 m teeth sections near (2.8 km fade) and a pale strip far (12 km). Varsity gliders round Hamminkeln and
Diersfordt; Wesel in ruins (A2). The three landmark bridges now turn to cross their river (GroundKit.river_axis).

## A8 1940 anti-invasion obstacles
Wikipedia - British anti-invasion preparations of the Second World War (open fields near the coast and airfields
obstructed with poles, trenches and old vehicles in summer 1940, cleared by 1942-43); Atlantic Wall (Rommelspargel,
1944). One merged mesh per obstructed field (poles grid + zig-zag spoil trenches), placed by the land-cover tiles in
fields within ~7 km of the sea: England 1940-06 .. 1942-12, the French / Belgian coast 1944-02 .. 1944-08. Max 6 per tile.
