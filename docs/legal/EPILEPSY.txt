PHOTOSENSITIVITY WARNING

A very small percentage of people may experience a seizure when exposed to flashing lights or visual patterns, including in video games. This can happen even to people with no history of seizures or epilepsy.

This game contains explosions, muzzle flashes, searchlights, lightning, rapidly changing light and screen effects.

Stop playing immediately and consult a doctor if you experience dizziness, altered vision, eye or muscle twitching, loss of awareness, disorientation, involuntary movement or convulsions.

Play in a well-lit room, sit a safe distance from the screen, and take a break of at least 10 minutes every hour. Do not play when drowsy or tired.
